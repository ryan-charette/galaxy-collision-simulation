# Code of Conduct

This project should be a practical, respectful place to collaborate on
scientific software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, constructive, and technically specific.
- Treat disagreement as part of improving the work.
- Critique code, data, assumptions, and methodology rather than people.
- Help new contributors understand project conventions and validation needs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Dismissing concerns without addressing the technical point.
- Publishing private communication or personal information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after maintainers ask for focus.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Include the
relevant links, screenshots, or context needed to understand the issue.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block accounts, or take other
reasonable steps to keep the project productive and safe.
